North-west India

Rajasthan

A little royal character. A thoroughly modern address.

Skip the palace. Start with a calm, neutral base, then let one terracotta surface, a jaali-inspired screen or a single piece of folk art carry the story.

A palette to start from

  • Terracotta
  • Sandstone
  • Brass
  • Block-print indigo
  • Terracotta and sandstone
  • Jaali-inspired details
  • Carved wood
  • Block-print textiles
  • Brass accents

South India

Kerala

A home that feels connected to nature, wherever you live.

Warm timber, cane and woven textures, soft linen, greenery and filtered daylight. Clean-lined furniture keeps it contemporary, and the materials do the talking.

A palette to start from

  • Teak
  • Cane
  • Palm green
  • Brass
  • Natural timber
  • Cane and weave
  • Soft neutrals
  • Greenery
  • Warm brass

South India

Tamil Nadu

Timeless craft, edited for the way we live now.

Rich woodwork, brass and natural stone, with geometric detail used selectively against a restrained palette. Depth and permanence without the heritage-set feel.

A palette to start from

  • Temple stone
  • Dark teak
  • Brass
  • Kanchi red
  • Rich woodwork
  • Brass details
  • Natural stone
  • Handwoven textures
  • Geometric motifs

South India

Karnataka

Local heritage, expressed with a contemporary point of view.

Warm timber, tactile stone and a touch of Mysuru-inspired craft. Keep the architecture clean and let a few meaningful details bring the story forward.

A palette to start from

  • Rosewood
  • Stone
  • Mysuru gold
  • Sandalwood
  • Warm timber
  • Stone textures
  • Mysuru-inspired craft
  • Brass accents
  • Art with a story

West India

West Bengal

Bengal’s artistic spirit, reimagined for modern living.

Art-led details, terracotta tones, warm wood and expressive textiles bring Bengal’s creative spirit into a contemporary home. Keep the furniture modern and let one striking artwork, a handcrafted object or a patterned textile tell the story.

A palette to start from

  • Terracotta
  • Jute
  • Deep green
  • Warm wood
  • Art-led interiors
  • Terracotta tones
  • Expressive textiles
  • Handcrafted details
  • Contemporary lines

South-east India

Andhra Pradesh

Let colour and artistry become part of your everyday backdrop.

Kalamkari-inspired art, earthy reds and indigo, carved wood and handcrafted objects. One meaningful piece makes a stronger connection than a room full of decoration.
